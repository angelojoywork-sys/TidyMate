Tidy mate: three visual directions

Same two screens, same content, same layout. Only colour and type change. Judge each one against the brief, not against taste:

  1. Calm first, playful second. Is the list quiet enough to scan in a second?
  2. The app does the talking. Does the overdue chore stand out from the brand colour without shouting?
  3. A house, not a competition. Does finishing a chore feel like a shared win?
  4. Vibrant, with black for text only. Does it feel lively on a white base?
