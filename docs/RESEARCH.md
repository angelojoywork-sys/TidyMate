# Research notes

Evidence gathered for design decisions, with sources and limits. Phase 6's competitor review builds on this.

## 2026-11-12: What the category looks like (for phase 1, visual direction)

**Question:** what colours and type do chore and roommate apps use, so we know whether a direction blends in or stands out?

**Method:** two App Store searches (US, iPhone), reading each result's icon and store screenshots at thumbnail size, plus one web search for which apps are established.

**Limits, stated plainly:**
- Mobbin was the intended source but its connector needs a paid plan, so no Mobbin screens were reviewed.
- The App Store began refusing requests after two searches. Individual app pages did not load.
- Store screenshots are marketing images at small size. Colour and overall tone are reliable; exact typefaces are not.
- Flatastic, Tody and OurHome are named by roundups as established, but their visuals were not seen. Only their feature positioning is recorded.

### Patterns

1. **Green and blue dominate.** They are the "clean" and "home" colours. The category leader seen is light sky blue.
2. **Purple is not rare.** At least three roommate apps use it.
3. **Many apps sell competition:** leaderboards, duels, points. Our principle "a house, not a competition" is a real point of difference, and the look should not read as a game (no neon, no scoreboard styling).
4. **Mascots with soft colour are common** (BoBo, Casamates). A mascot alone will not set us apart.
5. **Type is mostly plain or rounded sans.** Characterful heading type is uncommon, so type is the cheapest place to be distinctive.
6. **Several apps are dark.** A light, white-based interface already separates us from those.

### What this changed

- Direction A (Splash): confirmed that blue is common here. Its difference has to come from the deeper cobalt, the yellow accent and the Bricolage headings.
- Direction C (Grape soda): the earlier claim that violet is "the most distinctive" was wrong for this category and has been corrected on the comparison page.
- No green direction was added: it is the most used colour in the category.
- The three directions otherwise stand. None copies a competitor.

### Feature positioning (web roundups, mostly affiliate blogs, treat loosely)

- Flatastic: rotating chore plan plus shared expenses and shopping list, pitched at flatshares.
- Tody and Sweepy: cleaning organised by room and frequency.
- OurHome and Nipto: points and rewards, family-leaning.
